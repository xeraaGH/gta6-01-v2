PORTO VERDE — HOW TO PLAY

Story: 10 missions (M) across six districts — the docks, the casino, the stadium, the tower. Finish them all to become King of Porto Verde. Total purse: $12,400.
On foot: WASD moves, SHIFT runs, mouse aims, click shoots, F punches, 1/2/3 swap pistol / SMG / rifle.
Driving: E steals the nearest car (the law notices), W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, SPACE handbrakes, Q fires your lead's ability.
Wanted: 1–5 stars. Cruisers chase and PIT you, roadblocks appear at 3 stars, the helicopter hunts you at 4+. Break line of sight to cool off, or repaint at the Pay 'n Spray garage in the Docks ($150).
Side work: street races (R), taxi fares (T), 20 hidden packages ($250 each), district standing grows as you work a district.
Leads: C switches between Dante (Dead Eye slow-mo) and Lena (Nitro). Your save keeps cash, missions and standing.
Press H to close.
